## Add RuLawRetrieval (Russian legal article retrieval)

**Why this fills a gap.** MTEB has no Russian text retrieval task in the legal domain (the only `rus-Cyrl` + `Legal` task is the visual JinaVDR subset). Russian retrieval tasks today are RuBQ, RiaNews, RuSciBench and multilingual sets (MIRACL, mMARCO, MKQA), none of them legal. RuLawRetrieval covers the codes people search most often (Labour, Civil, Housing, Administrative Offences, Family, Consumer Rights Protection Law).

**Data.**
- Corpus: 3,786 articles of 6 codes of law from legalacts.ru, edition of September 2026. Official texts of laws are not copyrighted (Civil Code of RF, art. 1259 p. 6).
- Test: 728 queries in three styles (everyday situations without legal terms, short search queries, lawyer questions), balanced by type.
- Generation and checks: queries are generated by YandexGPT-5-Lite-8B-instruct from the article text and checked by an LLM judge of another family (Qwen3-8B), which rejected 12 of 1,039.
- Relevance: one relevant article per query. A 174-query golden subset (split `golden`) was re-annotated with multi-relevance.
- Dataset card with licenses, filters and slices: https://huggingface.co/datasets/alekseevpavel04/ru-law-retrieval

**Reference scores** (whole articles truncated at 512 tokens, the MTEB default; from the paper repo):

| model | ndcg_at_10 |
|---|---:|
| ai-forever/FRIDA | 0.861 |
| BAAI/bge-m3 | 0.847 |
| intfloat/multilingual-e5-large-instruct | 0.837 |
| intfloat/multilingual-e5-large | 0.828 |
| intfloat/multilingual-e5-small | 0.757 |
| BM25 (bm25s, Russian stemmer) | 0.673 |

Known limitations, stated in the dataset card:
- the queries are synthetic;
- there is one relevant article per test query; the golden subset shows that about 21% of queries have a second relevant article.
