## Add RuLawRetrieval (Russian legal article retrieval)

Retrieve the article of law answering a question, over 3,786 articles of 6 Russian codes (Labour,
Civil, Housing, Administrative Offences, Family, Consumer Rights Protection Law).

**Gap.** The legal domain is well covered in `mteb` — in every major language except Russian:

```python
legal = [t.metadata for t in mteb.get_tasks()
         if t.metadata.type == "Retrieval" and "Legal" in (t.metadata.domains or [])]
len(legal)                                             # 41, across 10 languages
[m.name for m in legal if "rus" in str(m.eval_langs)]  # []
```

Same shape as `AILAStatutes`, `GerDaLIR`, `LawIRKo`, `ZacLegalTextRetrieval`.

**Data.** Law texts are public domain (Civil Code art. 1259 p. 6). 728 test queries in three styles
(everyday / short search / lawyer), generated by YandexGPT-5-Lite-8B, verified by a judge of another
family (Qwen3-8B, rejected 12 of 1,039); queries naming the article number or copying 6+ consecutive
words are filtered. Card and licences: https://huggingface.co/datasets/alekseevpavel04/ru-law-retrieval

**Limitations** (in the card): queries are LM-generated — 7 text retrieval tasks already use the
same `sample_creation`; one relevant article per query, a re-annotated 174-query subset shows ~21%
have a second; single edition of the laws; `license="multiple"` — happy to change if another value
fits better.
